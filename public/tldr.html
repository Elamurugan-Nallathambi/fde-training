FDE training program / learning path / 12 weeks

From engineer or domain expert to forward deployed engineer.

Your own path through the program: pick your industry and one real project, choose how to build it, and work through 13 steps over 12 weeks. Each step saves evidence to your GitHub repository and the tutor checks it, so anyone you share it with can see exactly what you have done.
